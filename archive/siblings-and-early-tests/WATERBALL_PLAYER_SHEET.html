🥊 Form 1: Waterball Brute (Brawler Mode) Click ℹ️ for full compendium
Towering 10-foot water creature. Your AC increases to 15. You pummel enemies with heavy water blows and can knock them flat on their backs!
⚔️ Active Actions & Attacks Form-Specific & Universal
🥊 Slam Attack ℹ️ Action • 5 ft
Heavy fluid punch. +5 to hit. 2d6 + 3 bludgeoning damage. Target must pass DC 13 STR Save or fall Prone.
💣 Waterball Bomb ℹ️ Action • 15 ft
Detonate high-pressure blast (15-ft radius). Deals 2d8 damage and knocks targets 10 ft away on failed DC 13 save. (Costs 1 Power Charge).
🛡️ Shield Bash ℹ️ Attached Action
Slam forward while frozen to Lelouch's arm. +5 to hit, deals 1d6 + 3 bludgeoning damage and pushes target 5 ft back.
💨 Detach ℹ️ Action
Release from teammate's arm, dropping onto the floor into Brute or Puddle mode in an adjacent space.
⚡ Bonus Actions Pick 1 Per Turn
🔄 Change Shape ℹ️ Bonus Action
Instantly shift between Brute, Puddle, or Tag-Team Tool mode.
💚 Healing Surge ℹ️ Bonus Action
Spray soothing mist to restore 1d4 + 3 HP to yourself or your partner Lelouch. (Costs 1 Power Charge).
🔁 Swap Mode ℹ️ Attached Bonus
While attached to Lelouch, instantly toggle between Ice Weapon mode and Ice Shield mode.
❄️ Ice Spike Overcharge ℹ️ Attached Bonus
In Weapon Mode: Flash-freeze jagged frost crystals. Adds +1d6 Cold Damage to Lelouch's next weapon strike.
🧬 Passive Ooze Traits Always Active
🌊 Amorphous Body ℹ️ Passive
Squeeze through any crack, vent, keyhole, or pipe gap down to 1 inch wide without rolling any dice.
🫁 No Organs ℹ️ Immunity
Immune to breathing requirements, poison gas, vacuum space decompression, and drowning.
🤝 The Yuumi Rule ℹ️ Attached
While attached, you cannot be targeted by direct enemy attacks and you ride along on your partner for free!
⛸️ Puddle Slippage ℹ️ Puddle Only
In Puddle form, enemies have Disadvantage on attack rolls made against you when you slide past them.
